What changed from the first model

  • The “turret” was two different things. The 61.6 mm width was the Ø__DISCD__ movable disc, not the enclosure. The enclosure is a __ENCW__ mm square cage: a solid __FLOOR__ mm block with four __POST__ mm corner posts rising to __ENCH__ mm, chamfered on the vertical edges, with open windows on every face.
  • Both joints share one actuator stack (housing Ø__HOUSD__, harmonic-drive step Ø__PARTD__, recess Ø__INNERD__, bore Ø__BORED__, __ACTL__ mm long) plus a Ø__FLD__ four-screw flange behind it. At the shoulder the flange is fixed to the base; at the elbow it carries the forearm.
  • Link ends carry a six-screw hub pattern (R__HUBRB__, screws at link angle ± 30°) and the tip has a Ø__BORED__ bore with six R__TIPRB__ aux holes. Slots are __SLOTW__ mm wide, not 5.1.
  • Link length L1 = L2 = __L__ mm (was 99.4 / 103.8 at a different scale). Shoulder axis is higher: tilt-corrected, the camera looks down __PHI__°, so near-camera features read low.
  • Depth stack is now evidence-based (frame 2 horizontal axis, cross-checked by the frame 1 / frame 4 parallax), and it differs from the old assumption: the connector beam is on the camera side of both actuators and the forearm sits behind the elbow actuator.

Read this before trusting a number
